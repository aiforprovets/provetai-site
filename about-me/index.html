About me 

Dr. Amr El Serougy September 5, 2026

Veterinary AI & Data Specialist | Livestock & Poultry Practice

I bridge the gap between veterinary medicine and data-driven management for veterinary businesses across Egypt and the Middle East.

In a region where maximizing production efficiency and managing operational costs are critical, I empower veterinary enterprises to turn raw data into profitable decisions. Leveraging tools like SQL, Power Apps, Power BI, Excel, and Power Automate, I help businesses analyze their market performance, automate complex workflows, and streamline their daily operations.

Software tools only matter if they save animals and reduce costs. Because I work directly in the field with poultry, cattle, sheep, and rabbits, I design digital solutions rooted in real veterinary medicine—not just theory. I help livestock and poultry enterprises turn raw production numbers and field data into early disease warnings, lower mortality rates, and peak flock efficiency. 

By combining my clinical field experience in poultry, sheep, and rabbits with digital transformation strategies, I build practical, scalable tech solutions. My goal is to equip regional veterinary companies with the digital infrastructure they need to automate, innovate, and grow in today's competitive market.

Support Our Practice & Mission - Empowering Farm Veterinarians with AI Diagnostics

I am dedicated to the long-term research and development of AI-powered diagnostic systems specifically for livestock and poultry.

Because the majority of farm disease diagnosis relies on observing clinical signs, my mission is to build practical AI tools—like on-device acoustic monitors—that turn subtle symptoms into real-time, objective data. These systems are designed to directly support veterinarians in the field, helping them make faster, evidence-based decisions to improve animal welfare and significantly reduce antibiotic overuse.

Your subscription directly funds the ongoing hardware prototyping, AI model training, and field testing required to bring these diagnostic tools to life.

For backing the future of precision veterinary medicine, you can check my blog at https://boosty.to/aiforprovets
