Welcome to ProVet AI.

Dr. Amr El Serougy October 8, 2026

My name is Amr El Serougy, and I am a veterinarian with a strong passion for artificial intelligence and technology in poultry and livestock production.

I created ProVet AI to explore how AI can help veterinarians, poultry professionals, and livestock producers solve real-world challenges that we face every day while helping improve food production and food safety.

The goal of this website is simple:

To share practical AI ideas, tools, and applications that can improve work in poultry and livestock production.

Through ProVet AI, I will share:

  • Digital AI applications for poultry and livestock farms.
  • Ideas and projects that combine veterinary technology, AI, and IoT applications.
  • Low-cost practical solutions that can help veterinarians in their daily work.
  • Farm data analysis, decision-support solutions, and learning resources for veterinary data analysis.
  • Lessons learned while building and testing new AI projects.
  • Educational content about AI for veterinarians, students, and future veterinary professionals.

This website is also a place to document my journey of building AI solutions for the veterinary industry.

Some ideas may become working tools and applications in the future. Others may remain experiments. In both cases, I will share what I learn along the way.

I believe that the future of animal production will be supported by better data, better technology, and better decision-making.

If you are a veterinarian, researcher, student, or professional working in poultry or livestock production, you are welcome to join this journey.

ProVet AI is not only about technology. It is about understanding real farm challenges and exploring how practical AI solutions can support veterinarians, producers, and the future of animal production.

Thank you for visiting ProVet AI
